About the Event


Why Students Should Participate


It isn't just about competing; it is about stepping out of the classroom to test your limits. Participating gives you a unique chance to challenge yourself against peers from various institutions, solve modern engineering problems, and gain direct exposure to emerging technologies.


Symposium Objectives


  • To foster technical innovation and provide a platform for students to showcase their problem-solving capabilities.
  • To bridge the gap between academia and industry by encouraging hands-on engineering and development.
  • To promote collaborative learning and healthy competition among institutions nationwide.

Benefits of Participating


  • Official Recognition: Earn certificates of participation or merit to strengthen your academic portfolio.
  • Networking: Connect directly with industry experts, recruiters, and fellow innovators who share your passion.
  • Prizes & Rewards: Compete for massive cash prizes, trophies, and exclusive event goodies.

Non-Technical Events

Enjoy the lighter side of TechNova with entertainment, quizzes, treasure hunts and competitive gaming.

Photography

Capture moments that tell a story. Open theme photography contest.

Rules: Single image submission; original work only; basic editing allowed.

Registration Fee: Free

Prizes: Top 3 receive certificates and cash vouchers.

Quiz Competition

Team-based quiz covering general knowledge, tech trivia and puzzles.

Rules: Teams of 3; elimination rounds followed by finale.

Registration Fee: INR 100 per team

Prizes: Winners receive cash prizes and certificates.

Treasure Hunt

A campus-wide hunt of clues and challenges — teamwork and quick thinking rewarded.

Rules: Teams up to 4; follow event marshals' instructions; no external aids.

Registration Fee: INR 50 per person

Prizes: Winning team receives gift hampers and certificates.

Gaming Tournament

Competitive gaming across popular titles — individual and team brackets.

Rules: Players must register for specific game brackets; follow tournament schedule.

Registration Fee: INR 150 per player

Prizes: Cash prizes, gaming accessories and recognition.

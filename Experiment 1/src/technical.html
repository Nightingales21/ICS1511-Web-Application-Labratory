Technical Events

A curated set of technical competitions designed to challenge your ideas, coding skills, and engineering design. Below are the event descriptions, rules, and prize details.

Paper Presentation

Present innovative research ideas and findings to a panel of judges.

Rules: Maximum 12-minute presentation; 3-minute Q&A; single presenter or team of up to 3.

Prizes: 1st: Cash + Certificate; 2nd: Certificate; Best Paper Award.

Web Design Contest

Build attractive, responsive websites demonstrating UX and accessibility.

Rules: Teams up to 3; no external hosting required; submit zipped project.

Prizes: Winning team receives cash and project showcase opportunity.

Coding Challenge

Solve programming problems under time constraints across multiple difficulty levels.

Rules: Individual contest; standard input/output; allowed languages: C, C++, Java, Python.

Prizes: Top 3 winners receive cash prizes and certificates.

Project Expo

Showcase innovative projects with demos and poster sessions for judges and attendees.

Rules: Teams up to 5; bring working prototype or demo video; provide one poster.

Prizes: Best Project cash prize, runner-ups, and internship shortlists.
